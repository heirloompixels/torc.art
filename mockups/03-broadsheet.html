The Premise

Obsessed with the transmission of lived emotions

A contemporary art space focusing on experiment and experiential works, bringing cutting edge projects to life in the strange vortex which is Truth or Consequences, New Mexico.

A gallery in the middle of nowhere obsessed with understanding optimal ways to use art in the transmission of lived emotions from one human being to another.

We travel to remote places around the world and gather experiences which we turn into objects. We do not work separately from the rhythms of the natural world — we try to integrate with them, and to remember how small a part of it we are.

Truth or Consequences Contemporary is the physical space we occupy in our minds. A refuge for the arts supporting a laboratory to answer the questions of "what is relevant as we move into this new world."

This is a twelve-year creative run in this town, in four iterations: Mud Mountain Studio and 100th Monkey Gallery, with Olin West; Seba, a hip-hop influenced art space; Desert Archaic, a creative oasis; and Truth or Consequences Contemporary, which puts all of it together to carry rural art to an international audience.

Community Etching Press

Intaglio printmaking is one of the oldest forms of printmaking. This press enables artists to create stunning hand crafted prints, each of which is a unique work of art.

All artists take a two hour orientation. Six artists per block. Orientation $40 · Session $25 · Private time $20/hr.

Agile Meteor Press

Desert Town. Plenty of Water. Small beautiful books of unique stories for nomads out there on a rock between the big loving arms of the Milky Way.

One book per season, by invitation or submission. Eight titles to date.
