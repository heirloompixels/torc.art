Survey

Experiment and experiential works, in a strange vortex

Contemporary art space focusing on experiment and experiential works. We bring cutting edge projects to life in the strange vortex which is Truth or Consequences, New Mexico.

We travel to remote places around the world and gather experiences which we turn into objects. We do not work separately from the rhythms of the natural world — we try to integrate with them, and to remember how small a part of it we are.

Field note — Summer

We are in and out of the gallery this summer

As we do our annual Fieldwork — creating and rejuvenating out in the wild. Orders from the shop will ship slower than normal — please get in touch if you need a rush order.
