01 — Statement

Experiment and experiential works, in a strange vortex

Contemporary art space focusing on experiment and experiential works. We bring cutting edge projects to life in the strange vortex which is Truth or Consequences, New Mexico.

We are on the historic Main Street of a small desert town on the banks of the Rio Grande, in the foothills of the Greater Gila region.

02 — Fieldwork

We are in and out of the gallery this summer

Creating and rejuvenating out in the wild. Orders from the shop will ship slower than normal — please get in touch if you need a rush order.

03 — Building

Luchini Electric, standing since 1916

Built like a proverbial tank, it'll still be standing in 2116, 2216 and beyond. Kyle was struck by lightning while Jeannie belayed him rock climbing; that moment is now memorialized on our facade.
