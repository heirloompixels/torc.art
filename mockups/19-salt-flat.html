Statement

Contemporary art space focusing on experiment and experiential works. We bring cutting edge projects to life in the strange vortex which is Truth or Consequences, New Mexico.

We are on the historic Main Street of a small desert town on the banks of the Rio Grande, in the foothills of the Greater Gila region. Our building has stood since 1916 and will still be standing in 2116.

The gallery holds a standing selection of handloom, sculpture, intaglio, painting, printmaking, art clothing, adornment and knives, alongside books from Agile Meteor Press.

Fieldwork

We are in and out of the gallery this summer as we do our annual Fieldwork — creating and rejuvenating out in the wild. Orders from the shop will ship slower than normal; please get in touch if you need a rush order.
