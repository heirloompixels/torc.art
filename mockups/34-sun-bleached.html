Statement

Experiment and experiential works, in a strange vortex

Contemporary art space focusing on experiment and experiential works. We bring cutting edge projects to life in the strange vortex which is Truth or Consequences, New Mexico.

We are on the historic Main Street of a small desert town on the banks of the Rio Grande, in the foothills of the Greater Gila region. Our building has stood since 1916 and will still be standing in 2116.

Fieldwork — Summer

We are in and out of the gallery this summer as we do our annual Fieldwork — creating and rejuvenating out in the wild. Orders from the shop will ship slower than normal — please get in touch if you need a rush order. If you are making a trip, get in touch first and we will make sure someone is here for you.

Thank you, Jeannie & Kyle
