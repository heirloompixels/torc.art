Art in the Oasis

A Gallery in the Middle of Nowhere

A contemporary art space obsessed with understanding optimal ways to use art in the transmission of lived emotions from one human being to another.

Statement

Experiment and experiential works, in a strange vortex

We bring cutting edge projects to life in the strange vortex which is Truth or Consequences, New Mexico. You can visit us at 312 Main Ave.

We are on the historic Main Street of a small desert town on the banks of the Rio Grande, in the foothills of the Greater Gila region.

The gallery holds a standing selection of handloom, sculpture, intaglio, painting, printmaking, art clothing, adornment and knives, alongside books from Agile Meteor Press.

Fieldwork

We are in and out of the gallery this summer as we do our annual Fieldwork — creating and rejuvenating out in the wild. Orders from the shop will ship slower than normal — please get in touch if you need a rush order.
