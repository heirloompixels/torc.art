Monsoon

Struck, and then memorialized on the facade

Kyle was struck by lightning while Jeannie belayed him rock climbing. That dramatic and life changing moment is now memorialized on our facade — a signage piece paying homage to the monsoon season, which brings the desert to life and sustains it through the year.

This piece is a gift to our historic Main Street district — a welcome reminder that rebuilding our old buildings is the lightning helping to catalyze the formation of a strong and robust community.
