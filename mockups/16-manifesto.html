Truth or Consequences Contemporary

A Gallery in the Middle of Nowhere

Obsessed with understanding optimal ways to use art in the transmission of lived emotions from one human being to another.

· · ·

I. The Place

Contemporary art space focusing on experiment and experiential works. We bring cutting edge projects to life in the strange vortex which is Truth or Consequences, New Mexico — on the historic Main Street of a small desert town on the banks of the Rio Grande, in the foothills of the Greater Gila region.

You can visit us at 312 Main Ave, Truth or Consequences, NM 87901.

II. The Work

We travel to remote places around the world and gather experiences which we turn into objects. We do not work separately from the rhythms of the natural world — we try to integrate with them, and to remember how small a part of it we are.

Truth or Consequences Contemporary is the physical space we occupy in our minds. A refuge for the arts supporting a laboratory to answer the questions of what is relevant as we move into this new world.

· · ·

III. The Lineage

A twelve-year creative run in this town, in four iterations. Mud Mountain Studio and 100th Monkey Gallery, with Olin West. Seba, a hip-hop influenced art space. Desert Archaic, a creative oasis. And Truth or Consequences Contemporary, which puts all of it together to carry rural art to an international audience.

Our building at 312 Main St is historically known as the Luchini Electric Building, and has stood since 1916. Built like a proverbial tank, it'll still be standing in 2116, 2216 and beyond.

· · ·
