A Gallery in the Middle of Nowhere

Art in the oasis — Truth or Consequences, New Mexico.

A gallery obsessed with understanding optimal ways to use art in the transmission of lived emotions from one human being to another.

Contemporary art space focusing on experiment and experiential works. We bring cutting edge projects to life in the strange vortex which is Truth or Consequences, New Mexico. Visit us at 312 Main Ave.
