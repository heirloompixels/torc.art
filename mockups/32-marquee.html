Contemporary art space · Experiment & experiential works
Luchini Electric Building, 1916

We bring cutting edge projects to life in the strange vortex which is Truth or Consequences, New Mexico. We are on the historic Main Street of a small desert town on the banks of the Rio Grande, in the foothills of the Greater Gila region.

The gallery holds a standing selection of handloom, sculpture, intaglio, painting, printmaking, art clothing, adornment and knives, alongside books from Agile Meteor Press.
