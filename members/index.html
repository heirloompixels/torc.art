Members

Truth or Consequences Contemporary is a gallery in a town of six thousand people, in the poorest corner of New Mexico, showing work to anyone who walks in off Main Street. Some of what we make pays for itself. The best of it never will. Supporting membership exists to fund the second kind — in the words of the letter that started our first membership in 2021, “to fund artistic endeavors which are not commercially viable, but are necessarily imperative in their creation.”

We have run on this model since January 2021, when our project Intentionally Confusing began asking a worldwide network of micro-patrons to sustain experimental art directly. The thinking then still holds now: rather than performing for platforms that harvest the attention, we treat ourselves as the platform, and the people who value the work fund the work.

One account, one door

A membership is one account at members.torc.art that works across everything we build. There are no passwords — we email you a six-digit code, or you can use a passkey. Joining is free, and a free membership is a real membership.

What membership opens

  • Gather — our small community gathering place. Text first, images second; posts stand for seven days and then leave; no likes, no follower counts, everything signed. Any member can join and write while the first 144 seats are open; paying members keep the ability to write permanently.
  • Proofing — the voting field of the Periodic, our printed publication. Anyone can look; members vote, up or down, on the image treatments and written fragments laid out exactly as they would print. What the members choose is what goes to press.
  • The Periodic in your mailbox — the Periodic of Truth or Consequences, New Mexico is a hand-sewn, letterpress-covered zine growing into a quarterly tabloid newspaper. It is a paid-member benefit: as editions ship, paying members with a mailing address on file receive them in the post.

One thing membership does not buy: status. There are no public profiles, no badges, no visible list of who supports us. The work is the thing on display, never the members.

All plans are monthly. Payment is taken by Stripe — torc.art never sees your card — and your invoices, plan changes and cancellation all live in Stripe’s portal, where cancelling is exactly as easy as subscribing. If you stop paying, your membership stays: paying is a way to support torc.art directly, never a requirement.

What the money does

Membership money runs the unprofitable heart of this place: the community etching press priced at cost, exhibitions and performances in a town the art market has never heard of, the Periodic, and the slow business of paying artists fairly — the gallery pays out monthly, and takes the lowest commission we can manage. The gallery is our art project before it is a business, and members are how an art project keeps its doors open.
