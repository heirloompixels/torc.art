Fieldwork

Every summer the gallery gets quieter, because the gallery goes outside. Fieldwork is our name for the annual season of living and creating out in the wild — the practice this whole project grew from, and the one we return to each year.

We travel to remote places and gather experiences which we turn into objects. We do not work separately from the rhythms of the natural world — we try to integrate with them, and to remember how small a part of it we are. As we wrote in one of the first Dispatches, in 2021, after watching one of our favorite places on earth burn for nearly a month: “We realized, right then, that we had to go back to living by and in the wild, because the wild is as big as it will ever be in our lifetime right now. We are journeying to make art which is itself the creation; the journey is the destination.”

The habit is older than the gallery. Ten years before that dispatch we built a wooden teardrop trailer and lived in it for a year, making art and enjoying the wilds; before that we walked here, across the desert from Phoenix. The wilderness work is a joint, multidisciplinary project — painting, printing, aerial performance, stones carried home from riverbeds — and what comes back from a summer becomes the winter’s exhibitions and books. Evidence of Existence, a handbound codex of terra and flora prints from the American Rockies, was made entirely in the field in the summer of 2025.

While we are out

The gallery keeps irregular hours through the summer. If you are making a trip, get in touch first and we will make sure someone is here for you. Orders from the shop ship slower than normal — write to us if you need a rush order.
