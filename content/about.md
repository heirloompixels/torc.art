A gallery in the middle of nowhere, obsessed with understanding the optimal ways
to use art in the transmission of lived emotions from one human being to another.

We exhibit a wide variety of art from a diverse range of local, regional, and
national artists — a collection of treasures handmade in the desert.

## Who We Are

Truth or Consequences Contemporary is run by Jeannie Ortiz and Kyle Cunningham,
artists and adventurers who live in Truth or Consequences, New Mexico — moments
from the banks of the Rio Grande and in the foothills of the Greater Gila.

The current space at 312 Main Ave is the fourth iteration of art space we have run
in Truth or Consequences over the preceding dozen years. It opened in October 2023.

## Fieldwork

Each year we head out for Fieldwork — creating and rejuvenating in the wild.
