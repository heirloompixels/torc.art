Agile Meteor Press is the printmaking arm of Truth or Consequences Contemporary.
We publish editions, broadsides, and collaborative projects with the artists who
pass through the space.

## What We Print

- Limited-edition prints and portfolios
- Broadsides and ephemera
- Collaborative projects with visiting and represented artists

## Working With the Press

We take on a small number of collaborations each year. If you are an artist or
writer with a project suited to print, write to us at
[hello@torc.art](mailto:hello@torc.art) with a short description and images.
