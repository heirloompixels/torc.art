Agile Meteor Press is a project of Truth or Consequences Contemporary. It
reimagines book publishing in the 21st century, combining current printing
technology to make art books accessible to everyone with age-old craft for the
discerning collector.

The press has its own home at [amp.torc.art](https://amp.torc.art) — full
catalogue, submissions, and current titles.

## How We Print

Every title is made two ways at once. Larger runs are contracted to
[Bookmobile](https://bookmobile.com) in Minneapolis, Minnesota, printed
digitally. At the same time we craft special editions in house, bound by skilled
hands using ancient bookbinding techniques with the help of our community
volunteers.

Presales and the initial release are designed to recoup a title's production
cost, so it can roll forward into the next book.

## What We Publish

One book per season, by invitation or submission.

Almost all of our books are tangentially related to the physical location of
Truth or Consequences, New Mexico — though we take a broad view of what that
means, and follow no hard and fast rules about it.
