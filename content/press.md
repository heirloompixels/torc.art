# Press

## Smithsonian Magazine

June 9th, 2025 — [Smithsonian Magazine picks Truth or Consequences, New Mexico](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/travel/the-15-best-small-towns-to-visit-in-2025-180986681/) as one of the 15 best small towns to visit in the USA. TorC Contemporary is highlighted in the article for the art we have on view and our [community etching press](/etching-press/).

## Pasatiempo

November 7th, 2025 — [Better for verse: word comrades get support from a pair of fellow artisans](https://www.santafenewmexican.com/pasatiempo/better-for-verse-word-comrades-get-support-from-a-pair-of-fellow-artisans/article_ceba20c3-4a54-47d6-a28d-9c7436a89e85.html), on *Women of Consequences* and [Agile Meteor Press](/agile-meteor-press/), in the *Santa Fe New Mexican*.
