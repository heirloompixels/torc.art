<p>When we first met back in 2006 one goal of our life was to make art full time. In the USA, a place almost fully devoid of institutional support for the everyday artist this seemed like an impossible challenge, we knew that to do so we would have to live as simply as possible so as to be able to afford to keep making art.</p><p>In 2011 Truth or Consequences was chosen as a home base because we could buy a home for extremely little money and we love the desert and mountains in the surrounding area. It was not an illustrious place to move, especially coming from<a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2021/05/12/opinion/bozeman-montana-housing.html"> Bozeman, Montana</a> - but it allowed us to focus our energy solely on the creation of art.</p><p>This single minded focus on art has been successful -here we are 16 years later living the life we set out to find. We still DIY everything - car repair, home building, foraging and food preservation.  But we get to make art full time because we have TRULY AMAZING supporters of our work and visions - that is all of you we are talking about - THANK YOU.</p>
