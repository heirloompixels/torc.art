<p>Contemplating the arc of time in the places we exist.  At once the world seems fragile and yet all the more beutiful because it is so fragile. We look out at the constant change of the environment around us, the climate chaotic, plants and animals confused. We see all this and then look inwards at where we came from, the creatures we once were and teh possibilities they held. This place is both magical and disappearing.  </p><p></p><p>Permutations<br>Remembering time. <br>Spanning time<br>Timed cycles<br>Cycles<br>Cyclic<br>Introductiron<br>Forgotten Remembering.<br>Remembering</p><p></p><p>aknowledging cycles<br></p><p>We have been experimenting with film for about a year now and noticed this month that we now have a catalog of footage of events which transpired at this time a year ago. This aknowledgement of a yearly cycle reminds us all life is a series of cycles ever overlapping, evolving, growing and understanding love. <br><br>This world is changing just as we ourselves are changing, that, always, is for sure.    </p><p></p><p>Recognizing Cycles<br></p><p>Exploring the arc of time etched upon the places we exist. </p><p></p><p>This month brings new starts on our film projects. It's been about one year now since we really started to study how to make films. A year of experimentation constantly evolving our understanding of this new medium and pushing ourselves to see just what is possible in this realm. With this year mark we have now accumulated enough footage to be able to start to use ideas we shot at the same time the year before.  This history of film is a new and interesting experience to this project and opens up a whole new idea space of documenting time. </p><p>To date we have mostly done simple short films capture a single moment in time whereas now we are departing this format to explore creating rich layered stories exploring the whole world we live in.  This months film is our first short exploration into this new realm.  </p><p>Our thoughts drift towards meditations on time and place.  How to describe the world around us and the effects of the arc of time. The earth is changing in front of our eyes. This past month a truly massive fire has burnt most of the forest immediately around our home - the place we wander and explore and generate so much inspiration from.  Recognizing this is just but one event in a world chaotic and overflowing with change we contemplate how does our little story fit into the large puzzle which is the world we live in. </p><p>Our sketches in film are mere glimpses into the time we spend exploring places on this earth. They are subtle reminders we are present in the landscape, in the world of nature - moving through the homes and spaces of so many beings. Our impact is at once a curiousity and also a cataclysm. We enter slowly into the realm of these worlds asking just to be able to spend a moment in these places, but we have to drive to, burn oil, consuming that resource which is also hastening the demise of these environs we love so. If we are to be here in these places what are we to create in our time there. </p><p>Nature is the primary influence in all work we create - from Jeannie's weavings to Kyle's paintings - they all find their genesis in the wild spaces on earth.  With our explorations into video we aim to reconcile the dichotomy between being out in the open and working tediously in our studios.  The goal is to merge our art practice with the being in nature so that the influence is immediate and direct. </p><p>This summer marks a clean departure from the last years experimentation.  We are now focused on a broader project, having experimented and fermented ideas in our minds for the last year.  Now we are to describe the worlds we visit.  To bring them to a larger audience. And to document how they change over the time span of our short lives.  </p><p>Our films are taking on richer and more moody character.  They are not just about a moment, or a place, but the span of time which is our existence and our being in these places, ineracting and observing the dramatic changes unfolding every day. </p><p>It would be easy to fall into a rote documentary type formula for our films but this is something we have explicitly not gone the path of. Instead we are working intuitively, with no preconcieved notions of what we are creating, letting the act of working decide the outcome.  This way of working feels like the antithesis of our more formal art practices where a weaving is planned from the moment the loom is warped and a painting is realized by an image sketched. We have greatly enjoyed the last year of working in this way and have come to an understanding of the moving picture which we hope will be uniquely our own.   </p><p>The films are beget from a multitude of clips each a moment captured in time. These vignettes combine together to form the framework of an idea, an impresion. They interlink and unfold in rythmical approximations of an experience we shared and then in turn want to share with you. But the clips also exist all on their own. These clips are long photos capturing the nuance of a moment in a way the still never can.  We are keenly interested in figuring out a way to integrate these clips into our website and building a way to experience our films not just as a film but also as an interative online experience.  </p><p>  </p>
