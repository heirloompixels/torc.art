<p>Indeed I am on the right path. Always great to find these when scrambling around boulders and pokey cactus wondering if we are actually getting any closer to the base of a cliff or just going around in desert circles.  The mountains are getting crowded with cairns by people throwing up distractions just like they do in the real world, the key is to know how to recognize the real path and not fall victim to someone else’s fantasy.</p>
