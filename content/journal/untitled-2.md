<p>Hello everyone on this the evening of the final full moon of 2021.  </p><p>Winter is here in the Chihuahuan desert of southern New Mexico.  Our wood stove crackles, burning up the detritus of tree pruning and the wooden scraps of so many projects. The gardens are tucked in and cozy for the winter, garlic growing ever so slightly each day with the afternoon sun and lettuce oh so close to being ready for harvest in the cold frame.  Leaves are everywhere and a myriad birds have returned to this wintering oasis of the wing along the Rio Grande river.   </p><p>We have both been working hard and settling back into life in our studios. Jeannie is busy weaving and Kyle is busy painting.  It feels good to be back creating full time and renewed with new ideas and focus.  </p><p>We also are taking considerable amounts of time working away at the computer.  A bit of that work is ready for you all to see already.  We have both worked extensively on our websites and finally have great archives of our work available online.  Right now we are focusing of getting our most recent works up, and will continue throughout the winter to dive deeper and deeper in the photo archives. Our hope is to have a nearly exhaustive archive of both our works available by the coming of spring.  Please take a look at our sites: <a href="https://jeannieortiz.com">jeannieortiz.com</a> and <a href="https://kyleparkercunningham.com">kyleparkercunningham.com</a>.</p><p>We have also started to work more of the web presence of this intentionally confusing project.  You'll notice the websites design has changed and we are starting to flesh out more data.  We are starting to clarify a vision of just what this project is, with this we are better able to understand how to express our ideas through the website. This winter will see considerable more effort put into the intentionally confusing project as we solidify this experimental art and movement laboratory into a way of creating which will guide us towards future projects. </p><p>We are oh so thankful to those of you whom have chipped in and become a paid sustaining member of the project.  If you are a paying member be on the lookout for an email in the next week or two which lays out our intentions for the next year and what you can expext going forward.  Paid members are what will make this project work in the long run; you are all patrons of the arts helping us explore new possibilities in creation.  To thank you we have an exciting plan which will allow paid members the absolute best chance to acquire the other works we make.  This year we have tried various experiments and from those experiences we have a pretty solid plan on how to reward you for helping us!  </p><p>We hope you are all safe, healthy and happy.  We look forward to a brighter future and a new day full of boundless inspiration, to carry forwad with us all that we have learned and experienced and to allow it to become our beings and infuse our every creation. </p><p>We leave with you tonight with <em>"The Sun Sets" </em>a short filmed one evening high up in the mountains of Wyoming. </p>
