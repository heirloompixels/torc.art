# Manifest

This site is a collaboration. Jeannie Ortiz and Kyle Parker Cunningham made the gallery, the art and the years of letters, wall texts and announcements this site is built from. An AI assistant — Claude — built the site's structure and, on some pages, wrote the prose, working from the gallery's own archives.

This page lists every page and who wrote it. **gallery** means the words are Jeannie's and Kyle's, most of them carried over from the gallery's earlier pages and letters. **claude** means Claude drafted the page from those same archives, in their voice and quoting them wherever quotation marks appear. **gallery + claude** means their words with sections Claude drafted. Jeannie and Kyle are reading the drafts one by one, and a page becomes **gallery** when they have rewritten or approved it.
