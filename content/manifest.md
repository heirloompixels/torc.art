# Manifest

This site is a collaboration. Jeannie Ortiz and Kyle Parker Cunningham made the gallery, the art and the years of letters, wall texts and announcements this site is built from. An AI assistant — Claude — built the site's structure and, on some pages, wrote the prose, working from the gallery's own archives.

This page lists every page and marks, honestly, who authored its writing. **gallery** means the words are Jeannie's and Kyle's — most of it migrated from the gallery's earlier pages. **claude** means Claude composed the page, quoting the gallery's own documents where quotation marks appear. **gallery + claude** means their copy with sections Claude added.
