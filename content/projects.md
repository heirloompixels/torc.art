# Projects

The gallery is a building on Main Street, and it is also a constellation. Some of these projects hang on the walls, some arrive in your mailbox, and some live entirely at addresses under torc.art. All of them are the same experiment: understanding optimal ways to use art in the transmission of lived emotions from one human being to another.

## In the building

- **[Exhibitions](/exhibitions/)** — rotating shows of local, regional and national artists, plus the Second Saturday Art Hop that has anchored our calendar since 2012.
- **[The Community Etching Press](/etching-press/)** — printmaking and bookbinding equipment held in common, priced at cost, with a barter program for anyone the prices would stop.
- **Performance** — the gallery doubles as a listening room. Tatsuya Nakatani — a Truth or Consequences neighbor and a great percussionist — conducted a sixteen-gong orchestra of sixteen volunteer musicians here in March 2026, and dance has run through the space since the grand opening, where six dancers performed. A dedicated address exists for joining the Nakatani Gong Orchestra: [NGO.torc@gmail.com](mailto:NGO.torc@gmail.com).
- **Workshops & Education** — letterpress printing, printmaking, drawing, natural dyeing; taught by us and by guest teachers, to children and adults. The teaching thread here is as old as the gallery itself.
- **[Glyphs](/glyphs/)** — leave your own mark on a 1916 building that will outlast us all.

## On paper

- **[Agile Meteor Press](/agile-meteor-press/)** — our publishing imprint. One book per season, printed by Bookmobile in Minneapolis for the trade editions and bound by hand in house for the special ones. Its own home is [amp.torc.art](https://amp.torc.art).
- **The Periodic** — *the Periodic of Truth or Consequences, New Mexico*: hand-sewn zines in letterpress covers, growing into a quarterly tabloid newspaper on the solstices and equinoxes. The first quarterly closes Volume I at the winter solstice, December 21st, 2026. What prints is chosen by the members, at [proofing.torc.art](https://proofing.torc.art).
- **The Dispatches** — the numbered newsletter we wrote from 2021 into 2024, thirty-some installments of art, wilderness and this town, is coming here from the retired intentionallyconfusing.com. Not yet built; watch this space.

## Around the town

- **[MeTeORiC](/meteoric/)** — the free arts and film festival, returning in 2027.
- **[The facade](/about/)** — the cloud-and-lightning signage on the Luchini Electric Building, our gift to the historic Main Street district.

## On the network

- **[Shop](https://shop.torc.art)** — the work, for sale. Handloom, sculpture, intaglio, painting, printmaking, art clothing, adornment, knives and books.
- **[Members](/members/)** — one account at [members.torc.art](https://members.torc.art) for everything we build, and the way supporting members fund the art that can't fund itself.
- **[Gather](https://gather.torc.art)** — a small community gathering place. Posts stand for seven days, then leave. No likes; all signed.
- **[Proofing](https://proofing.torc.art)** — the Periodic's voting field, where members choose what goes to press.
- **Futures** — a market for a thousand works that do not exist yet. Beginning at the winter solstice of 2026, we will make a new corpus of 1000 works across one full turn of the year — paintings, drypoints, jewelry, handmade books, marble sculpture, weavings — released into a live market at [futures.torc.art](https://futures.torc.art) while they are still being made. A thousand numbers, each number its own price, one dollar to a thousand. Whoever holds a work when the market closes in late 2027 is who it ships to. The board has not opened yet; it opens December 21st, 2026.

## And the artists' own archives

The people behind this gallery keep public catalogues raisonnés of their own work — [archive.jeannieortiz.com](https://archive.jeannieortiz.com) and [archive.kyleparkercunningham.com](https://archive.kyleparkercunningham.com) — because a gallery in the middle of nowhere had better keep good records.
