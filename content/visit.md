# Visit

Truth or Consequences Contemporary<br />
312 Main Ave<br />
Truth or Consequences, NM 87901

We are on the historic Main Street of a small desert town on the banks of the Rio Grande, in the foothills of the Greater Gila region.

## Hours

We are in and out of the gallery through the summer while we do our annual Fieldwork. If you are making a trip, get in touch first and we will make sure someone is here for you.
