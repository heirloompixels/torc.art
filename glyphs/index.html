Glyphs

The world over people have left their icons incised into the rocks. The glyphs and emojis of the ancients bring their lives into our own. We see them as people living lives in a place and a time.

Our project as artists is to make marks which talk to the world we live in and the world which may exist tomorrow.

The icons project embraces this long tradition of image creation by providing a marquee location in TorC to leave your own glyph.

The Luchini Electric Building at 312 Main St has existed since 1916; built like a proverbial tank it’ll still be standing in 2116, 2216 and beyond.

Get in touch if you’d like to leave a mark.
