Calendar

Upcoming events located upon the temporal timeline of this particular entity’s existence, and a record of those already passed.

Future

The MeTeORiC festival, originally set for February 20th, 21st and 22nd 2026, has been postponed until 2027. We're very sorry.

Past

March 20th, 2026 — Nakatani Gong Orchestra
A 16 gong orchestra conducted by Tatsuya Nakatani, performed at the gallery. Sixteen volunteer musicians joined the orchestra after a day of rehearsal.
March 14th, 2026 — Jeannie Ortiz opening reception
Opening of What Hands Do, a body of new works in new mediums.
February 14th, 2026 — Art Hop: Tatsuya Nakatani Bow Show
A special one night event showcasing the stunning bows of the great percussionist Tatsuya Nakatani.
December 13th, 2025 — Art Hop
Mainstreet Truth or Consequences Second Saturday Art Hop.
November 14th, 2025 — Women of Consequences release party
6–8pm at the gallery main space, 312 Main Ave, TorC NM.
November 8th, 2025 — Art Hop
Second Saturday Art Hop.
November 7th, 2025 — Pasatiempo
Article on Women of Consequences and Agile Meteor Press released.
July 20th, 2025 — Launch of Agile Meteor Press
Our publishing imprint opens its doors. More.
July 12th, 2025 — Art Hop: signage illumination
The inaugural illumination of our new art signage installation at 8:45pm, during the Mainstreet Truth or Consequences Second Saturday Art Hop.
June 9th, 2025 — Smithsonian Magazine
Smithsonian Magazine picks Truth or Consequences, New Mexico as one of the 15 best small towns in the USA. TorC Contemporary is highlighted in the article for the art we have on view and our community etching press.
February 21st–23rd, 2025 — MeTeORiC
The MeTeORiC festival, across venues all over town.
February 24th, 2024 — Grand Opening: Home
The Grand Opening Ceremony of Truth or Consequences Contemporary at 312 Main, held on the Saturday night of MeTeORiC 2024, with the inaugural exhibition Home — dance, poetry, a DJ, and a performance by Hieronymus Bogs.
February 23rd–25th, 2024 — MeTeORiC
The festival returns, with Tatsuya Nakatani performing twice in one evening.
December 7th, 2023 — the first pARTluck
A gathering to eat food, discuss, and make art together — potluck + art. The evening marked the opening of the renovated space, twelve years to the day since we arrived in Truth or Consequences.
August 18th, 2023 — 312 Main St
We are moving to a new space. Opening October 2023.
October 2019 — the first MeTeORiC
The inaugural MeTeORiC festival: some thirty artists across Truth or Consequences.

Weekly open printing sessions at the Community Etching Press run most Saturdays.
